Every subject in the catalogue appears here, whether or not this student has taken it, so the crosses are as plain as the ticks. Inside a subject the marks come from the topic results: a tick once the topic is passed against the syllabus pass mark, a question mark while it is still being studied. A section is passed when everything under it is, and carries its tally either way. The percentage on a subject is passed topics over the topics its syllabus makes assessable — marks are entered under Subjects, or on the student subject row itself.
